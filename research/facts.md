# NMF donasjonsside: faktagrunnlag (research 2026-10-12)

Confidence: **high** = primary source, number quoted directly; **medium** = primary source but indirect/regional/needs rounding; **low** = secondary or non-Norwegian source, or client claim unverified.

## 1. Origin of marine litter on the Norwegian coast

- Source split by count, national, 2024: personlig forbruk 38 %, industri og næring 23 %, fiskeri og havbruk 18 %, annet/uidentifiserbart 20 %, hygiene 2 %. Hold Norge Rent, Rydderapporten 2024 (https://holdnorgerent.no/content/uploads/2025/04/HNR-Rydderapporten-2024.pdf), 2025. **high**
- Same split 2023: forbruk 38 %, fiskeri og havbruk 31 %, industri 14 %. Rydderapporten 2023 (https://holdnorgerent.no/content/uploads/2024/12/Rydderapporten-2023.pdf), 2024. **high** (year-to-year swing shows the split is noisy)
- Five-year average 2021–2025 (16 873 aksjoner, >1.1 mill. funn): forbruk 34 %, fiskeri og havbruk 26 %, industri og næring 22 %; i.e. næringsrelaterte kilder ≈ 48 %, consumer ≈ 34 %. HNR, "Rydderapporten: fem år med folkeforskning" (https://holdnorgerent.no/content/uploads/2026/04/HNR-Rydderapporten-femarmedfolkeforskning.pdf), 2026. **high**
- By weight: "Fiskeri, havbruk og maritim sektor er den største bidragsyteren til marin forsøpling i Norge, målt i vekt." Most frequent identifiable item = bøtekapp (short rope offcuts from net repair). Handelens Miljøfond / SALT national monitoring 2024, press release (https://kommunikasjon.ntb.no/pressemelding/18644226/norges-verste-strandsoppel-er-menneskeskapt-med-kniv?lang=no), 2025. **high** (no percentage given)
- Norwegian vs foreign: "Nærmere 60 % av gjenstander som kan indikere opprinnelsesland kommer fra Norge og naboland." Same HMF/SALT 2024 release. **medium** (only items with a readable origin; most litter has none)
- Miljødirektoratet: "Det meste av forsøplingen nasjonalt kommer fra norske kilder og i mindre grad fra andre land." (https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/ansvarsomrader/avfall/plast-i-havet/). **high** (qualitative)
- Deep-dive nationality of packaging (SALT "Kvantesprang", rapport 1060, 2022, https://holdnorgerent.no/content/uploads/2025/05/KVANTESPRANG.pdf): Agder: unknown 36 %, Norway 29 %, Nordic 15 %, other Europe 12 %. Møre og Romsdal: unknown 45 %, Norway 19 %, Nordic 14 %, UK 7 %, other Europe 9 %. Troms og Finnmark: unknown 49 %, Norway 14 %, Nordic 11 %, Russia/CIS 12 %, other Europe 8 %. Maritime sources (fiskeri, akvakultur, shipping) ≈ one third of items by count in these three counties, higher by weight; Indre Oslofjord only 4 %. **high** for the numbers, **medium** as a national picture.
- Barents Sea: 40–60 % of plastic litter is fisheries-related (Strand et al. 2021, Sci. Total Environ.; via High North News https://en.highnorthnews.com/science/plastic-on-norwegian-beaches-comes-from-nearby-ocean-areas/127151). **medium**
- Verdict on client claim "60–70 % Norwegian": roughly right if phrased as "about 60 % Norwegian or Nordic among items whose origin can be read; the rest UK, Russia and other Europe; a third to half of packaging cannot be traced at all". Do not claim 70 %.

## 2. Age of the litter

- NRK, Værøy (Lofoten) 2017: "En halv meter ned i sanden, finner vi 40–50 år gammel plast" (June Grønseth); Freia sjokoladesaus-flaske with a decades-old label. (https://www.nrk.no/nordland/_--en-halv-meter-ned-i-sanden_-finner-vi-40-50-ar-gammel-plast-1.13668956), 2017. **medium** (anecdotal, no formal dating)
- SALT Kvantesprang 2022: "strandsøppel består av både nytt og gammelt søppel i alle fylkene". Møre og Romsdal: of datable packaging 26 % older than 5 years vs 11 % younger (63 % undatable). Agder: 22 % vs 21 % (56 % undatable). Oil/chemical cans show the widest age range (dated by production date); foreign litter in Agder is on average older than Norwegian. **high** (no share for the 1970s is published anywhere)
- NMF Levende Hav 2024 report: "flere lag med gamle presenninger som lå dypt begravd i sand og jord" (Kaupanger). (https://nmf.no/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Levende-Hav-Strandryddeturne-2024-komprimert.pdf), 2025. **medium**
- Degradation: PET bottle ≈ 400–450+ years (Plastic Change / Håll Sverige Rent, cited via skagenonline.dk FAQ). **low** (no Norwegian authority publishes a number; Miljødirektoratet only says plast "sakte fragmenteres" and can lie "i hundrevis av år").
- Verdict on "a large part dates from the 1970s": **unverified, replace**. Safe wording: "Vi finner fortsatt plast fra 70- og 80-tallet" (sourced to NRK 2017 + SALT), not a percentage.

## 3. Composition

- Plastic share 2024: 68 % of weight was plastic; 78 % incl. EPS/XPS. Five-year: "7 av 10 funn er plast". HNR 2024 / 5-year report. **high**
- Top 5 by count 2024 (235 193 funn): uidentifiserbare plastbiter 18 %, EPS (isopor) 10 %, tau 9 %, sigarettsneiper 8 %, snusposer 6 %; top 10 = 68 % of all finds. 72 % of rope pieces are under 5 cm (bøtekapp). HNR 2024. **high**
- Presence on clean-ups 2024: tau on 49 % of actions, plastflasker 44 %, matemballasje 42 %, EPS 39 %. HNR 2024. **high**
- Five-year by count: tau 11 % of finds, present on 57 % of actions; 75 % of rope < 50 cm. HNR 5-year. **high**
- By weight (SALT Kvantesprang 2022): "Trålkuler, nett og olje- og kjemikaliekanner er relativt fåtallige, men veier mye. Tau er både tallrike og relativt tunge." Rope > 1 cm diameter carries most rope weight; one trawl bag in Møre og Romsdal weighed 360 kg (79 % of that sample). Large items (> 100 cm) dominate weight in Troms og Finnmark. **high** (qualitative; no national %-by-weight table exists publicly)
- Honest copy: "En typisk kilo norsk strandsøppel er plast, mest tau, garn, isopor og kanner fra sjøen, pluss flasker og emballasje fra land." Any precise %-by-weight split would be an example number.

## 5. Yield and cost per volunteer-day

- National volunteer averages 2024: 278 kg per aksjon, 17 frivillige per aksjon, 15 kg per frivillig; 2023: 289 kg, 19 people. HNR 2024. **high**
- NMF 2024: 33 028 kg over 151 ryddeaksjoner = ≈ 219 kg per action; 111 km coastline, 0.3 kg/m; 18 long-term volunteers + staff + 4 102 pupils. NMF Levende Hav 2024. **high**
- NMF boat-day range: "bare 100 kg per dag" on a clean stretch (Alver) vs 1.6 tonn from 600 m (Trollholmen, Austrheim), >1 tonn in one action with KV Tor. NMF LH 2024. **high**
- Professional benchmark: Plastjegerne AS ≈ 1.2 tonn per day on average (Møre og Romsdal). HMF 2024 release. **medium**
- Cost per kg (Prospera for HNR, "Verdien av frivillig opprydding", 2025, https://holdnorgerent.no/content/uploads/2026/03/Analyse-Verdien-av-frivillig-opprydding-HNR.pdf): professional Rydd i Tide 2020–2025: 540 MNOK / 4 mill. kg = **135 kr/kg**; 2024: 115 MNOK / 675 000 kg = **≈ 170 kr/kg** (3 150 kr/time); volunteer clean-up societal cost **36 kr/kg** (173 kr/time); municipal valuation of volunteer work ≈ 101 kr/kg; societal benefit 680–1 400 kr per kg cleaned. **high**
- Waste handling only: refusjonsordningen for eierløst marint avfall pays a guide rate of **2,50 kr/kg** to waste companies (Miljødirektoratet via HNR, Kriterier 2026, https://holdnorgerent.no/content/uploads/2026/05/Refusjonsordningen-for-eierlost-marint-avfall_Kriterier-2026.pdf). **high**
- Plausibility of client figures: 600 kg/day with 10 people + 2 boats = 60 kg/person, 4× the national volunteer mean but within NMF's own boat-day range on hotspots (100 kg to 1 600 kg). **Plausible as a good day, not an average; label "en god ryddedag".** €1 000 ≈ 11 500–12 000 kr (EUR/NOK ≈ 11.5–11.8, 2026, approx.) → ≈ 20 kr/kg all-in with volunteer labour. That sits between the 2,50 kr/kg disposal rate and the 36 kr/kg societal volunteer cost, and far below the 135–170 kr/kg professional rate. **Credible.**
- "10 kr = 1 kg": the client's own numbers give ≈ 20 kr/kg, so 10 kr/kg is optimistic by 2×. Defensible only if framed as "hver tier du gir dekker omtrent en halv til en kilo" or if NMF confirms a cheaper day (e.g. fuel + disposal only ≈ 200 € + 600 kg × 2,50 kr ≈ 3 800 kr ≈ 6 kr/kg). Recommended: use **"20 kr = 1 kg"** (sourced to NMF's own day) or keep 10 kr but call it "ca." and footnote.

## 6. Funding rule tied to member count

- Regulation: Forskrift om tilskudd til frivillige klima- og miljøorganisasjoner samt klima- og miljøstiftelser, FOR-2021-06-11-1928, Klima- og miljødepartementet, last amended FOR-2024-01-10-54 (https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2021-06-11-1928). **high**
- § 2 d: eligible organisations must have "et register over minst **5000 tellende medlemmer** (3000 for barne- og ungdomsorganisasjoner)". Also: democratic bylaws, Frivillighetsregisteret, broad geographic membership. **high**
- § 5: first-time applicants must have existed ≥ 4 years. § 8: grant share = points: members (in thousands) × membership income (MNOK), max 150 points, + 100 base points + 1 point per lokallag (min 5, max 200) + 10 per fylkeslag (max 100). A tellende medlem has paid ≥ **50 kr** kontingent for the year and has full democratic rights from age 15. § 7: 77 % of the pot goes to member organisations, 23 % to foundations/umbrellas. **high**
- NMF confirms the threshold bites: "vi pr i dag med i underkant av 3200 medlemmer ikke er berettiget statsstøtte etter regjeringens endring som medførte at organisasjoner med mindre en 5000 medlemmer ikke vil få vurdert sine søknader om støtte" and "siste år, uten statsstøtte". NMF Årsmelding 2020 (https://app.innsamlingskontrollen.no/storage/document/arsberetning-norges-miljovernforbund-2020.pdf). **high**
- Note: NMF's 50 kr student/pensioner rate is exactly the § 8 minimum, so those members count. Family members under one kontingent count only if ≥ 100 kr per family.
- Momskompensasjon (Lotteri- og stiftelsestilsynet) and Frifond are not member-threshold schemes for an adult environmental NGO; not checked in detail. **low / not verified**
- Verdict: client's belief is **correct**: NMF needs ≥ 5 000 paying members to be considered for state grunnstøtte at all; above that, the amount scales with members × membership income.
